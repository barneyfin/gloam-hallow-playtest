# Gloam Hallow playtest

A playtest copy of **Gloam Hallow**, a game by Lantern Box Games, for friends and family to try. Not finished.

Open https://barneyfin.github.io/gloam-hallow-playtest/ and choose:

- **Play Gloam Hallow** (`gloam-hallow.html`): the game. A run is saved at each lantern, so a closed tab can carry on from the title (Continue). Since 0.33.0-r2, The Endless Rot, a mode of endless waves in small arenas, is open from the start: choose it after Start game. Since 0.35.0-r2, the game has a difficulty setting: once something is bought at the Echo Shrine (or with The Endless Rot chosen), Start game shows Shallow, Grim and Agony under the runs; The Bare Reckoning is always Grim. On Grim and Agony monsters hit harder and take more hits place by place, get a little faster with each place, drop less, and the heal spell costs more mana; the echo kept scales with the setting. The first time a device opens this version its high scores, personal bests and any saved run are cleared once (the stolen echo and everything bought stay), because the game is harder than the one those scores were made on. Since 0.36.0, the bosses fight back two new ways. Stand at a boss's feet too long and it knocks you back: a steady ember glow spreads round it for 0.7 s, then a burst throws you away from it, further on harder settings and at later bosses (get out of the glow to dodge it). And at marks on its bar it mends: it goes still in the mana bar's colour, your sword glances off, and only an attack spell that lands stops it. The Veiled Ghost does neither on Shallow or Grim in the first loop; the other bosses do on every setting. The first time a device opens this version its high scores, The Echo Infusion's best and any saved run are cleared once more (The Endless Rot's bests, the stolen echo and everything bought stay). The speed test waits longer at each boss, to time both moves, so it runs about half a minute longer. Since 0.36.0-r1, Night has health potions of its own lying on its path: on Grim (and so The Bare Reckoning) one at the start and one at the boss's door, on Shallow the door's only, on Agony neither. Since 0.36.0-r2, Night's own potions are in the first loop only and lie there again after a life lost (each ahead of the lantern you come back at), and the bosses mend more: from one mend of 10% to three of half the bar, by boss, setting and loop (each mark on the bar still mends once a fight). Since 0.36.0-r4, on Agony Night's monsters hit and take blows as on Grim in the first loop (a crawler's or a flyer's hit 10% and a mouth-hat's 15%; a crawler falls to 2 blows, a flyer to 1 and a mouth-hat to 3), so Agony's harder monsters start at Blood Red Rain; the second loop's Night is as it was. Since 0.36.0-r3, Night's own potions are on Agony too (at the start and at the boss's door, as on Grim), and Personal bests shows The Echo Infusion as it shows The Endless Rot: a best for each setting played, under the run's name (the one best kept before no longer shows). Since 0.33.1, the forest's leaves drift smoothly however long you've played, and on a computer the ; key beside L rolls as Shift does. Since 0.34.3, the walkers and ghouls walk on stepping legs, a walker's stooping sweep hits you standing (roll through it), a crawler's lunge or a flyer's dive no longer turns you round, and the wave's number is easier to read. Since 0.45.0 (the latest build): in an app that draws a page's text larger by the phone's font size, the game's text keeps its drawn size. Nothing else changes here: this copy plays as 0.36.0-r4 did. Since 0.45.0-r1, two changes to words: in the choice of run the row under the runs reads "Difficulty:" whichever run is chosen, and the Echo Shrine's footer has no key hint (Backspace still goes back). Since 0.45.1, one more: on the win's card, the button that starts the second loop reads "Go round again (harder)". Since 0.45.1-r1, this copy has three more pieces of the finished game: the Echo Shrine's second page, Cosmetics (a glow for each belt, 450 echo, once its belt is owned; lantern glass, 300 echo; both show on the wanderer in the next run); a bought look kept at Picture: Fastest (the belt's glow as two plain lines, and his light); and the backgrounds painted again if the device's graphics restart in play. The speed test still runs in the plain look.
- **Speed test** (`gloam-hallow.html?check`): Start game plays by itself for about two minutes, then shows how smoothly it ran, with Email, WhatsApp and Copy to send the result (with the last 20 runs played on this device).
- **Send my runs** (`gloam-hallow.html?runs`): the last 20 runs played on this device (how far each got, the lives lost and how it ended), with Email, WhatsApp and Copy to send them. The game sends nothing by itself.

`gloam-hallow.html` is a built single-file page (made by `python lantern-box/kit/build.py gloam-hallow` in Barney's arcade). The fonts embedded in it, IM Fell English and Alegreya Sans, are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; their licences are in `licences/`. The music was made with Google Gemini.
